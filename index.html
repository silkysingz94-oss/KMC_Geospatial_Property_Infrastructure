Methodology
1. Retrieve published Gambia administrative boundary metadata.
2. Download the LGA and district GeoJSON geometries.
3. Use the selected LGA geometry to establish the query extent.
4. Query OpenStreetMap features through Overpass API.
5. Filter returned infrastructure against the selected boundary.
6. Calculate feature counts and road feature totals from the returned data.
Road statistics in this dashboard are feature counts, not road length or road density. Road length and density require a suitable projected coordinate system and additional processing.
